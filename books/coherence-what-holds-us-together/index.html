The book in five minutes

COHERENCE is where the idea was first written down. It tells, in an emotional and narrative way, what coherence is: the invisible structure of people and stories that keeps a person steady.

It introduces the Coherence Wheel, the rings of one, four, fifteen, one hundred and fifty and beyond, and the idea of drift: the slow loosening that happens when those rings thin and nobody notices.

The Village Came First is its expanded, revised successor. Start here if you want the first and most personal telling.

Key takeaways

  • Before you could speak, someone held you. That is the first fact of every life.
  • Your life is held up by rings of people, each doing a different job.
  • Drift is quiet. Most of us notice it late, as tiredness or loneliness.
  • Coherence can be returned to, with other people.
