The book in five minutes

This is the companion to The River and the Salt. If that book builds the instruments of clear thinking, this one describes what they are up against.

The fog is outside: feeds built for outrage, misinformation that makes truth feel irrelevant, the pull to agree with the group. The mirror is inside: performing a self we have not earned, biases that defend what we already believe, contradictions we carry to protect who we think we are.

Written to you, in short and plain sentences, it ends each chapter with a moment of reflection, and offers one protocol for the worst moments: one breath, one true thing, ten seconds.

Key takeaways

  • Much of the noise you feel is designed. It is not a personal failure.
  • Your attention is harvested. You can learn to notice the harvest.
  • Performing a life costs more than living one.
  • When it is too much: one breath, one true thing, ten seconds.

Who it is for

Read this if you end most days more scattered than you started them.

You may notice the pull a few seconds earlier. That is often enough.

From the book

You have already been pulled today. Probably more than once. Not dramatically. Just the ordinary version.

What this book does not claim

It does not tell you to quit your phone or the internet.
