The Signal ≠ Value Lab

A thumbs-up is a signal. It is not the value.

The same person gives different feedback when they feel held and when they do not, so a signal carries an error that depends on their state. The Signal ≠ Value Lab lets you see that gap in an interactive simulation, and shows the derivation and the pre-registered test behind it: h = v + ε(state), where h is the feedback given, v is the value the person holds and ε(state) is an error that depends on their state when they answered.

What is inside

  • The derivation of h = v + ε(state), the basis of Coherence-Weighted Human Feedback (CWHF).
  • A five-layer model of how feedback gets contaminated.
  • An interactive simulation of personas and states.
  • The pre-registered hypothesis H1 and the result that would falsify it.

Questions

Is the lab evidence that CWHF works?

No. It illustrates a pre-registered hypothesis. No empirical data has been collected, and the numbers in the simulation are illustrative.
