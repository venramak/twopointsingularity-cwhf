The Signal ≠ Value Lab

A thumbs-up is a signal. It is not the value.

The lab separates what is known about the gap between what people signal and what they value, what is being tested, what a full simulation of the test shows, and what is only proposed. Every claim carries its tier.

The lab runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing. Open it full screen ↗

Read the paper (PDF)Code on GitHub ↗

What is inside

  • Evidence: 71 cases, each tagged with one of eight reasons a signal departs from a value.
  • Reach: where human signals (thumbs, votes, upvotes, clicks) enter AI systems, and how far a result can travel.
  • Flip test: answer 12 questions rushed and at your own pace, and see which way you drift. A demonstration, not data.
  • Simulation: the full study run on simulated participants, including four design flaws it caught.
  • Proposal, Method and Claims: CWHF v2 (record, prevent, weight, audit), definitions, and every claim with its status.

Questions

Is the lab evidence that CWHF works?

No. It sets out a testable hypothesis and the design to test it. No human data have been collected, and the simulation shows only how the study would behave.
