The Neev app

Draw who holds you.

The Neev app opens with a short coherence audit. You answer a few questions and see, in your own words, who holds you and in what ways. It is not therapy and not a personality test. It shows that people need people in different ways. Your answers stay on your phone.

How it fits

  1. Watch. Neev, a five-part Hinglish series, tells the story of why humans believe.
  2. Draw. The app turns the idea into your own map of the people who hold you: four, fifteen and a hundred and fifty.
  3. Test. The Signal ≠ Value Lab shows what a test of the claim would look like.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No login is needed.

Where do my answers go?

They stay in your own browser or phone. See the privacy notice.

Is it therapy?

No. It is for reflection. If you are in distress, contact a qualified professional or a helpline such as Tele-MANAS (14416 in India).
