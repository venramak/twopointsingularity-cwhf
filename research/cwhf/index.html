Coherence-Weighted Human Feedback

A person no one is holding answers differently from the same person at their steadiest. Feedback used to train AI carries the value a person holds plus an error that depends on their state:

h = v + ε(state)

Current training treats that error as zero. CWHF proposes weighting feedback by the coherence of the person who gave it.
